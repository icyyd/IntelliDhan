How this paper test works

A breakout of the first 15-minute range must retest that level and agree with the 9-period moving average and the session's volume-weighted average price.

Entries use the observed ask plus modeled slippage; exits use the bid minus modeled slippage. Fees are included. Stops are triggers, not guaranteed fill prices.

This is an unvalidated research strategy. Paper fills do not prove real-world execution or profitability.
